Under the Hood

Four layers of validation

01
🔤

Syntax Check

Validates the email format using regex. Checks structure, allowed characters, and proper domain format.

02
🌐

Domain Check

Queries DNS to confirm the domain exists and has MX records — meaning it can actually receive emails.

03
📬

SMTP Check

Connects to the mail server and asks if the specific mailbox exists — without sending any email.

04
🛡️

Risk Rating

Identifies role-based addresses (info@, admin@) and suspicious patterns that indicate low deliverability.
